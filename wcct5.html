About Us

Every day, thousands of stray animals and birds suffer silently from hunger, accidents, and neglect. At Weathercraft Charitable Trust, we refuse to look away. Our trust operates as a lifeline for street dogs, street cows, vulnerable goats, and injured birds. Through immediate medical intervention, structured feeding programs, and dedicated shelter spaces, we transform pain into hope.
