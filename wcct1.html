About Weathercraft Charitable Trust

Every day, thousands of stray animals and birds suffer silently from hunger, accidents, and neglect. At Weathercraft Charitable Trust, we refuse to look away. Our trust operates as a lifeline for street dogs, street cows, vulnerable goats, and injured birds.

Through immediate medical intervention, structured feeding programs, and dedicated shelter spaces, we transform pain into hope. We give a voice to the voiceless, ensuring every creature receives the love, care, and protection they deserve.
