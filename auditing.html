Audit Assistance

Our Audit KPO services provide accounting firms, CPA firms, and businesses with comprehensive audit support solutions designed to improve efficiency, reduce turnaround time, and enhance audit quality.Our experienced professionals assist audit teams by handling time-consuming audit procedures, documentation, and analytical tasks, allowing auditors to focus on complex areas and client relationships.

Audit planning support - risk assessment, materiality determination, audit programm preparation.

Working paper preparation and documentation to firm-specific templates

Substantive testing — confirmations, vouching, tracing, analytical procedures

Internal control evaluation and documentation (including flowcharting and narratives)

SOX Section 302/404 control testing and documentation support

Compilation and review engagement preparation (SSARS compliance)
